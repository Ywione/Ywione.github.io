Source and download formats

Government of Bermuda, Department of Statistics. Quarterly Bulletin of Statistics — Annex I, Q1 2026. Official publication information. The workbook was supplied to this site’s author through the publication mailing list.

Time-series downloads use one observation per row, with numeric values, frequency, measure, classification, index base and source references. Percentage changes are kept separate from levels. Original-layout downloads retain the published headings and notes. These extracts do not recalculate or repair the original workbook’s formulas. Grey source shading means “Not available for updating”; the corresponding cell references are listed in the download notes and table metadata.

Table extracts and web presentation by Ywione Darrell. Statistical source attribution remains with the Department of Statistics and the agencies named in each table.
