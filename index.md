I am a 2026–2028 ODI Fellow at the Bank of Somaliland, where I work as a Monetary Policy and Research Expert. Previously, I worked as a Consultant Analyst in the Financial Stability and Research Department at the Bermuda Monetary Authority from July 2025 to October 2026. My research interests include income inequality, small island developing states, offshore financial centres, Dutch Disease and cross-border financial flows.

I hold an MSc in Economics for Development from the University of Oxford and a First Class BSc in Political Economy from King’s College London.

I am the Co-Founder and Co-Chief Editor of the [Bermuda Journal of Academic Research](https://bermudajournal.org).

## Research interests

- Income inequality
- Small island developing states
- Offshore financial centres
- Dutch Disease
- Cross-border financial flows

I make Bermuda data series from the Bermuda Department of Statistics available as downloadable Excel workbooks and CSV files on the [Datasets]({{ '/datasets/' | relative_url }}) page.
