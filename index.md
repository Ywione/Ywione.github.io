I am a macroeconomic research consultant in the Financial Stability Research Division at the Bermuda Monetary Authority. My research interests include income inequality, small island developing states, offshore financial centres, Dutch Disease, cross-border financial flows and firm dynamics.

I hold an MSc in Economics for Development (Merit) from the University of Oxford and a First Class BSc in Political Economy from King’s College London.

I co-founded and co-edit the [Bermuda Journal of Academic Research](https://bermudajournal.org), and I co-founded Longtail Learning Bermuda, which supports educational outcomes for Bermudian youth.

## Research interests

- Income inequality
- Small island developing states
- Offshore financial centres
- Dutch Disease
- Cross-border financial flows
- Firm dynamics
