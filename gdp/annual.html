Definitions and comparability

Real GDP uses 2013 prices. The 1996–2020 history retains an unknown publication vintage; 2021–2025 comes from the latest annual report. 2025 is provisional.

Calculated growth compares selected annual levels; published growth uses each report’s comparison and can differ. The supplied 2007 real GDP figure differs from the rebasing note by BD$0.072 million and is retained.

Annual industry GDP and quarterly expenditure GDP are different measures; annual values need not equal the sum of quarters.

Time series

One row per observation: date, series, value and units. Dates mark period ends. Use the Measure filter to view growth rates; the initial view shows levels and indices. Missing observations are omitted. A percentage value of 5 means 5%.
