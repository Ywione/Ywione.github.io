Definitions and comparability

Real GDP at 2006 and 2013 prices is kept in separate series, including overlapping quarters; the two series are not spliced.

Nominal GDP combines release vintages. Published growth is retained from the selected release, not recalculated from the combined history.

All quarterly observations are provisional. The Q1 2026 release flags imports and related aggregates as not comparable with earlier releases; GDP includes these aggregates.

Time series

One row per observation: date, series, value and units. Dates mark period ends. Use the Measure filter to view growth rates; the initial view shows levels and indices. Missing observations are omitted. A percentage value of 5 means 5%.
